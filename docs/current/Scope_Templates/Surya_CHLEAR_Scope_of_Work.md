## 7. Influencer Marketing

### 7.1 Ideation

**Description:**

- Concept and theme generation for influencer content
- Aligned to campaign messaging pillars
- Content angle recommendations matched to each campaign phase:
  - Teaser
  - Pre-Launch
  - Launch

### 7.2 Script & Creative Direction

**Description:**

- Script writing for each influencer content piece
- Creative direction notes covering:
  - Tone
  - Key messaging points
  - CTA
- CHLEAR's scope begins at the script stage onward
- Shoot, posting, and on-ground coordination handled by the influencer / their team

### 7.3 Commercial & Scope Note

**Not included in this scope:**

- Influencer identification
- Influencer outreach
- Influencer negotiation
- Ongoing relationship management

**Fees note:**

- Individual influencer fees are not included in the retainer or campaign pricing
- Individual influencer fees are contracted and paid directly by the Client
